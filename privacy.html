Undiyal privacy policy

App: Undiyal - Family Budget (package in.sridhar.undiyal) · Effective date: 7 October 2026

In short: Undiyal is an offline family expense and income tracker. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Who we are

Undiyal is made by an individual developer in India. Contact: support.undiyal@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

  • The records you enter: expenses, income, amounts, dates, notes, categories, family member names, accounts, budgets, recurring entries, templates, bill reminders and their history.
  • Receipt photos or files that you choose to attach to an entry (copied into the app's private storage).
  • Settings, such as reminder times and your automatic backup choices.
  • If you turn on the app lock: a salted, one-way hash of your PIN (never the PIN itself). Fingerprint or face unlock is handled entirely by Android; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint or face data.
  • If you buy Premium: a record that Premium is active, encrypted with a key that stays inside your phone's secure key store, so Premium works offline.
  • If the app ever crashes: a technical crash report (error details, app version, phone model and Android version; no amounts or names from your records).

What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

  • Backups and exports. When you create a backup or export to CSV or Excel, or set up automatic backup, the file is saved to a place you pick (for example your phone's storage, an SD card or a cloud storage app). Anyone with a backup file can read it, so keep it safe. If you pick a cloud storage app, that service's privacy policy applies to the file.
  • Crash reports. Saved on the phone only. They are shared only if you tap "Share" in More > Diagnostics and choose how to send them.
  • "Send us a note". Opens your own email app with our address filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send it. We use emails you send only to reply to you, and delete them on request.

Purchases (Premium and tips)

Premium and tips are optional and are sold only through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google, not by Undiyal; see Google's privacy policy. The app asks Google Play which purchases your Google account has, so Premium can be turned on, restored on a new phone, or turned off after a refund. Google gives the developer limited order information (for example an order number, the product, price, date and country) so refunds and support requests can be handled. The developer does not receive your card or bank details.

Information collected by Google Play

Like every app on Google Play, Google may collect technical information such as installs, crashes and app performance, according to your device settings and Google's own privacy policy. This is done by Google Play, not by Undiyal.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: bill reminders, the daily expense reminder, budget alerts and automatic backup problems. You can turn them off.
  • Start after restart: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Fingerprint or face: only if you turn on fingerprint unlock for the app lock.
  • Google Play billing: to buy Premium or leave a tip through Google Play.
  • Standard background-work permissions added by Android's libraries (for example keeping the phone awake briefly while a backup is being written). None of them gives the app access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or messages.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Undiyal contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking code, does not create user accounts, and does not sell or share personal data with anyone.

Keeping your data, and deleting it

  • Your data stays on your phone until you delete entries in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Backup and export files you saved elsewhere stay until you delete them.
  • Because the developer holds none of your records, there is nothing for the developer to delete. For anything else, write to support.undiyal@gmail.com.

Security

Records are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can add a PIN or fingerprint lock. Backup files are not encrypted, so store them somewhere safe.

Children

Undiyal is a family budgeting tool meant for adults. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Changes that affect how your data is handled will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.

Contact
