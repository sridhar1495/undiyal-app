Undiyal - Family Budget

An offline family expense and income tracker for Android. Record what each person spends and earns, set monthly budgets and see where the money goes. Everything stays on your phone: no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Undiyal (உண்டியல்) is the Tamil clay coin pot that families use to save, one coin at a time.
